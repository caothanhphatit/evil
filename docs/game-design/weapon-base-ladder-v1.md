# Weapon Base Ladder (v1)

This is rebuild-designed content. Names, unlock bands, and visual themes below
are not claimed as original-package mappings.

Each base unlocks at the start of a 100-level band and caps at the end of that
band. Eight difficulties therefore produce eight current bases per class:
`0-99` through `700-800`.

English and Vietnamese are required for every rebuild-designed weapon. Stable
IDs and asset names never contain localized display text.

## Class silhouettes

- **Berserker:** heavy two-handed blade or axe, broad asymmetric silhouette,
  visible impact weight, no shield.
- **Paladin:** two-handed hammer or maul, architectural head, balanced holy
  geometry, not a one-handed mace.
- **Ranger:** practical bow evolving into a large recurved war bow; readable
  string and limbs at icon size.
- **Sorcerer:** staff or scepter with a distinct magical focus; avoid turning
  every tier into the same crystal-on-a-stick shape.
- **Dark Knight:** spear, glaive, lance, or halberd; long dark silhouette with a
  readable blade and counterweight.

## Asset direction

- `24 x 24` logical pixel art enlarged by exact nearest-neighbor `4x` to a
  `96 x 96` RGBA delivery PNG.
- Weapon fully visible with the class-specific orientation defined in
  `weapon-asset-style-bible-v1.md`.
- Strong silhouette first; small engravings must not carry item identity, and
  generated concept art must be reconstructed on the logical pixel grid before
  integration.
- No text, character, hands, scenery, frame, rarity border, watermark, shadow,
  or reflection in the source asset.
- Final assets use a transparent canvas and live in a separate rebuild content
  release; package-derived icons remain immutable.

## Localization contract

- Required launch locales: `en`, `vi`.
- English is the source writing locale; Vietnamese is reviewed product copy,
  not a machine-translated fallback.
- Missing required localization fails catalog validation.
- Asset paths use stable IDs such as `wp_ranger_400.png`; changing display text
  must never rename an asset or invalidate player inventory references.
