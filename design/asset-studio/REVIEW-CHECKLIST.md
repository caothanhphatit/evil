# Asset Review Checklist

## Evidence

- [ ] Evidence label is present and correct.
- [ ] References are cited with paths or screenshot identifiers.
- [ ] No unresolved mapping or gameplay meaning was invented.

## Family Consistency

- [ ] Canvas, crop, scale, projection, and safe area match the family brief.
- [ ] Silhouette, outline, palette, lighting, and detail density match anchors.
- [ ] The asset remains readable at actual game size.
- [ ] Variants differ only in fields declared variable by the brief.

## Technical Quality

- [ ] Correct dimensions, color space, format, and alpha policy.
- [ ] No clipped edges, halos, stray pixels, watermark, text, or AI artifacts.
- [ ] Transparent pixels and edge colors are safe for atlas extrusion.
- [ ] Source master and generation metadata are preserved.

## Runtime Promotion

- [ ] Canonical ID and provenance are recorded in the asset manifest.
- [ ] Transformations are scripted and deterministic.
- [ ] Browser-size derivative and actual-size visual review pass.
- [ ] Licensing/provenance review permits use.
